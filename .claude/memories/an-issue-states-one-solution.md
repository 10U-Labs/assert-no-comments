# An issue states one solution

An issue names one change — this function, this file, this dependency — and
what the choice rests on. Never file "either X or Y", a menu with a
recommendation, or a question left for the reader, and leave out the options
that lost and the reasoning that ranked them.

**Why:** the issue is the instruction to whoever picks it up, and they were not
in the conversation that produced it. The options that lost are the trail to
the answer, not part of it, and a reader has to get through them to find it.

**How to apply:** settle the choice before filing, whatever form the issue
takes per [[issues-have-no-fixed-form]]. When a later finding settles a
question an open issue was weighing, rewrite the body to give the answer and
cut every sentence the finding makes moot.
